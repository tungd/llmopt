LFM2.5-350M Q8 cost-model device sweep evidence
Date: 2026-08-26
Target: isolated Q8 linear dispatches from the LFM2.5-350M cost-model harness
Host: arm64 Mac16,8, macOS 26.6.1, 16 GPU cores
Runtime: native PyTorch MPS bridge; parameterized generated Metal kernels

Implementation gate:
- `python/tests/test_metal_runtime.py`: 5 passed.
- `ninja -f ninja.build metal-runtime`: passed after the float32 entry-point mapping fix.
- The bridge keeps `llmopt_q8_linear` for `(16,16,64)` and dynamically loads
  `llmopt_q8_linear_tm{Tm}_tn{Tn}_tk{Tk}` for the seven generated variants;
  float32 input selects the corresponding `_f32` function.

Device datasets:
- `device-dataset.jsonl`: 640 rows, 16 `(M,N,K)` shapes, 8 tile variants,
  5 samples per variant.
- `device-dataset-25.jsonl`: 3,200 rows, the same 16 shapes and variants,
  25 samples per variant.
- `device-dataset-broad.jsonl`: 8,960 rows, 224 shapes over
  `M={1,2,4,8,13,16,32,64,128,256,512,1024,2048,4096}` and
  `N,K={512,1024,2048,4096}`, 5 samples per variant.

Selected raw observations from the first dataset:
- `(M,N,K)=(1,1024,1024)`: fixed median `273.75 us`; best measured tile
  `8x32x128` at `169.75 us` (`-37.99%` relative to fixed).
- `(13,1024,1024)`: fixed median `169.667 us`; best `8x32x128` at
  `145.25 us` (`-14.39%`).
- `(13,2048,1024)`: fixed tile is the measured winner at `188.875 us`.
- Across the broad data, the exhaustive measured oracle has 21 of 112
  `M=2..64` shape groups at or above the declared 15% fixed-tile delta; its
  mean oracle delta is `7.30039710966991%`. This is an oracle observation,
  not a learned-selector result.

Numerical probe:
- Shape `(13,1024,2048)` was dispatched through all eight kernels and
  compared with the MPS dequantized `F.linear` reference.
- Every variant, including the fixed baseline, reported `exact=false`,
  `max_abs=0.0625`, and `mean_abs=1.3649463653564453e-05`.
- The result is therefore recorded as common native Q8 drift, not exact
  cross-tile FP16 equality.

Offline model probe:
- The 24-estimator/depth-2 model trained on the 640-row dataset has
  held-out `R2=0.9569819197141608`, portable max relative error
  `1.4329246283350704e-07`, and generated OCaml source size `13,355` bytes.
- Its exhaustive top-1 tile selection on the held-out measurements is
  `1/16 = 0.0625` for the recorded hardware metadata, M4-Pro configuration,
  and default-device checks.
- The generated model was not copied into `lib/kernel_cost_model.ml` because
  the selection result does not satisfy the supporting decision's declared
  95% top-1 criterion.

Evidence boundary:
The parameterized dispatch and isolated measurements are real device
observations, but the current trained selector does not meet the declared
selection criterion and no full-model static-versus-dynamic Q8 linear speedup
claim is made from these artifacts.
