Compiler change
---------------

`Pass_fuse_rms_norm` now unwraps a single-use f16-to-f32 input cast after
forming `Rms_norm`; `Pass_fuse_swiglu_ffn` expresses the same optional cast in
the declarative rule and carries the original f16 activation into the fused
region. `Fusion_query` therefore has typed `cast` and input-level `or`
patterns. `Pass_fuse_rms_rope` accepts the direct f16 RMSNorm form as well as
the legacy casted form so the existing rope fusion remains selected.
