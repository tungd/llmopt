Change
- Before: each generated schedule kernel created, committed, and synchronously waited on its own Metal command buffer.
- After: one OCaml execution schedule encodes its compute kernels and typed copies into one ordered Metal command buffer, then commits and waits once.

- Cache pack/unpack dispatch outside execute_schedule remains independently submitted.
