LFM2.5-350M Q8 relative-target cost-model repair
Date: 2026-08-26
Target: LiquidAI/LFM2.5-350M Q8 weight-only tile sweep

Repair:
- `python/llmopt_backend/cost_model/train.py` now supports a
  `relative_delta` target. For each `(M,N,K)` shape, the target is the
  measured tile latency divided by the measured fixed `16x16x64` latency,
  minus one. The fixed-tile row is required for every shape, and repeated
  samples must be aggregated before training.
- The installed artifact is
  `_artifacts/cost-model-repair-2026-08-26/model-relative-delta-16x3.json`:
  16 trees, depth 3, learning rate `0.05`, seed `23`. Its generated predictor
  is composed with the existing typed OCaml selector in
  `lib/kernel_cost_model.ml`.

Grouped validation:
- The median dataset has 1,792 rows over 224 shapes and eight candidate tiles.
- A seed-23 holdout keeps all eight tiles for each of 45 held-out shapes and
  trains only on the other 179 shapes. Fixed `16x16x64` measures
  `164409.671 us`; the model-selected tiles measure `163824.794 us`, a
  relative reduction of `0.003557436715508102` (`0.3557436715508102%`). The
  model selects `16x16x64` for 33 shapes and `8x32x64` for 12, matching the
  measured winner for 25/45 shapes.
- Five grouped folds over all 224 shapes sum to fixed
  `731266.884 us` and model-selected `728923.884 us`, a relative reduction of
  `0.00320402858554732` (`0.320402858554732%`), with measured winners on
  133/224 shapes. No shape's rows cross a train/test boundary.
- Receipts: `_artifacts/cost-model-repair-2026-08-26/relative-model-validation.json`
  and `_artifacts/cost-model-repair-2026-08-26/static-vs-relative.json`.

Boundary:

but it does not establish the LOOP's declared `>= 15%` Q8-linear speedup or
