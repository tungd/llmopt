## Root cause and repair

The v3 `kernel.metal` and `kernel.metallib` contain the four LM-head argmax
functions, including the f32 variant, but the v3 `package.llmopt` did not
declare those ABI entries. In `q8_entries`, the pipeline operator made the
LM-head append conditional on the absence of residual-norm entries, so a graph
containing both macro families omitted the LM-head manifest entries.

`lib/metal.ml` now binds the residual-norm append before independently appending
the LM-head entries. `test/test.ml` adds a mixed residual-norm plus LM-head
graph assertion for `llmopt_q8_lm_head_argmax_f32` with `Float32 -> Int32` ABI.

Offline verification passes:

- `ninja -f ninja.build test`: 43 Python tests and the OCaml test suite pass.
- `ninja -f ninja.build all metal`: pass.

## Corrected static v4 pair

The corrected packages are in
`_artifacts/lfm25-350m-q8-relative-model-2026-08-26-v4/{prefill,decode}`.
They were generated after the single native attempt; they were not dispatched
on the device.

| Stage | Kernels | Commands | Opaque | Macro operations |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Prefill | 96 | 699 | 0 | 16 residual-norm, 16 dual-linear, 6 QKV, 1 LM-head argmax |
| Decode | 93 | 719 | 0 | 16 residual-norm, 16 dual-linear, 6 QKV, 10 ShortConv steps, 1 LM-head argmax |

The ABI-14 serving-pair check passes grouped-Q8 width 64, page size 1, six
prefill templates, six decode-past templates, and specialized decode metadata
of 659 commands, 13 runtime inputs, and 6 paged-attention operations. Against
the 795/815 baseline, the static reduction remains 96 per stage and 192
combined. The v4 package manifests now contain
`llmopt_q8_lm_head_argmax_f16`, `llmopt_q8_lm_head_argmax_f32`, and both
secondary-logit variants.

## Gate boundary

The native attempt produced no token IDs, no full-model TPOT, and no fresh
four-request parity result because the v3 package failed at kernel lookup.
The v4 static repair closes the manifest defect, but the macro ITEM-05 and
ITEM-06 device gates and the XGBoost ITEM-06 `>= 15%` Q8-linear gate remain
open under the one-attempt boundary. Existing cost-model grouped validation
still measures `0.3557436715508102` on the 45-shape holdout and
`0.320402858554732` across five grouped folds, below the LOOP's declared
`>= 15%` condition.
