# LFM2.5-350M Q8 v4 macro-fusion fast probe results (post-optimization)

Date: 2026-08-26
Target: `LiquidAI/LFM2.5-350M`, Q8 weight-only, full macro fusions with on-GPU token-id argmax

## Optimizations Applied
1. Caching normalized inputs in threadgroup SRAM (`threadgroup float cached_norm[2048]`) in `llmopt_q8_lm_head_argmax_f32`, eliminating 67,108,864 redundant DRAM transactions per token.
2. Vectorizing inner dot products across all 4 macro kernels (`llmopt_q8_lm_head_argmax_f32`, `llmopt_q8_linear_add_norm_extra_f16`, `llmopt_q8_dual_linear_silu_f16`, and `llmopt_q8_qkv_linear_f16`) with 4-way `char4` loads and single outer scale multiplication.
3. Intermediate activation caching in `llmopt_q8_linear_add_norm_extra_f16` to eliminate the redundant duplicate GEMV computation per layer.
4. Threadgroup SRAM input caching in `llmopt_q8_dual_linear_silu_f16` and `llmopt_q8_qkv_linear_f16`.
5. Removing barrier pipeline flushes in `native/ocaml_metal_stubs.m` under serial compute command encoding (`MTLDispatchTypeSerial`), eliminating 175 pipeline drain stalls per token.
6. Correcting radix cache reporting in `bin/lfm_serve.ml` using `Generation.Driver.State.cached_prompt_tokens`.
